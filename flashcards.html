About review dates & local data

Remembered cards return after 1 day, 7 days, then every 30 days. “Again” resets a card to a 10-minute retry. These are simple practice intervals, not a mastery assessment.

Dates stay in this browser when local storage is available. Moving files, switching browsers, or clearing browser data may lose them. Private browsing or local-file restrictions may prevent saving; a warning will appear. The dashboard and cards work without a server, but a consistent local-server address gives more predictable storage.

Flashcards support recall; architecture also requires practice and feedback. Bring uncertain answers or real code examples to your teaching agent. Card ratings do not unlock lessons or change learning records.
