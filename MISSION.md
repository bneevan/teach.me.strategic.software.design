# Mission: Strategic Software Design

## Why
Develop the judgment to design, evaluate, and evolve maintainable software systems, and strategically guide AI coding agents rather than merely generating working code.

## Success looks like
- Diagnose complexity with concrete evidence and predict the effects of a change.
- Design deep modules with clear interfaces, hidden decisions, and localized knowledge.
- Discover domain models and ubiquitous language, enforce invariants, and choose bounded contexts deliberately.
- Compare architectural alternatives, explain trade-offs, and evolve a system safely.
- Give AI agents constrained tasks and evaluate their designs, tests, and changes independently.

## Constraints
- Integrate Ousterhout, Hunt & Thomas, and Evans rather than teach three separate books.
- Present the complete curriculum first; teach one short lesson at a time and evaluate responses before advancing.
- Use coding, design challenges, code review, active recall, Socratic questioning, and spaced repetition.
- Programming experience, preferred language, and available study time are not yet established.

## Out of scope
- No additional exclusions established yet.
