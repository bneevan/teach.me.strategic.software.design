Knowledge

  • John Ousterhout: A Philosophy of Software Design, second edition. Author's book page and edition notes; use for complexity, strategic investment, deep modules, information hiding, comments, and design alternatives. The page was inspected during setup. The linked second-edition extract is a scanned PDF, not text-extractable here; do not imply its contents or the full book have been verified from this page. Assign readings by chapter title, not unverified quotations or page numbers.
  • John Ousterhout: CS 190 Introduction, Winter 2024. Author's public lecture notes, inspected for Lesson 2. The “Complexity” section defines complexity through difficulty understanding and modifying software, names dependencies/inconsistencies/special cases, and explains why modular design hides complexity. Use as the short primary reading for diagnosis. Lesson 2's detailed diagnostic questions and stale-rule example are instructional synthesis, not quotations from these notes. Companion APOSD discussion outline confirms the course's focus on interfaces, dependencies, information hiding, and making code obvious. The current author book page supplies further reading.
  • Hunt & Thomas: The Pragmatic Programmer, 20th Anniversary Edition. Publisher page with verified table of contents and official extracts; use for orthogonality, reversibility, tracer bullets, contracts, testing, and refactoring. This edition is the course baseline.
  • Official extract: DRY—The Evils of Duplication. Read during setup. Distinguishes duplicated knowledge from merely similar code and describes maintenance as continuous; primary reading for Lesson 1. Independent concepts may coincidentally have the same representation.
  • Eric Evans: DDD Reference, direct PDF. Author's public pattern summaries, derived from the original book with later additions explicitly marked; definitions and contents inspected during setup. Use for ubiquitous language, model-driven design, bounded contexts, aggregates, and strategic design. This is a reference, not a replacement for the book's full explanations. Summaries licensed CC BY 4.0; attribute Evans when adapting them.
